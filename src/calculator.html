Rules this page uses

  • Friction loss: one rule, C x (GPM/100)² for each 100 ft. C is 0.08 for 5 inch hose and 0.2 for 4 inch hose. Dual 3 inch lines use 0.8 on half of the flow in one line.
  • PDP: always rounds up to the next 5 PSI. A demand of 31 PSI gives 35. A demand of 30 PSI stays 30. The minimum is 50 PSI.
  • Climb: 0.5 PSI for each foot, as the chart shows.
  • The hose keeps a minimum of 10 PSI at each point on the route.
  • LDH limit: 200 PSI. The page warns when a downhill run goes above it.
  • 3 inch hose limit: 250 PSI.
  • Hose load: one engine lays each hose section. No section is longer than the supply hose on one engine.
  • Reverse lay: each engine starts at the attack end of its section and lays hose back to its pump position.
  • Forward lay: each engine starts at the source end of its section and lays hose to its position.
  • Ground heights: USGS 3DEP LiDAR, 1 metre grid, read every 25 ft.
  • Hydrants: Westchester County GIS, dated October 2015, and Town of Greenburgh, 2026. The data has no flow values.
  • Not included: the flow the water source can give, appliance loss, and attack line pressures.
